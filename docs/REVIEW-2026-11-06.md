# Whole-application review, 6 Nov 2026

Six read-only reviews run in parallel over the API, the web till and admin, and the desktop app: barcodes and the scanner, the ledger (khata, double-entry finance, shifts, day close), order and payment money paths, security and sync, inventory, reports, canteen and payroll, and the till's offline robustness. Each claim marked CONFIRMED was traced through the exact code path or reproduced with a scratch request spec against the local test database (the probe specs live outside the repo). Nothing was changed by this review. The top items were re-read by hand afterwards.

Paths are relative to the repo root. Severity: **critical** = money or data can be taken or lost by an outsider; **high** = money, stock or a sale goes wrong in normal use; **medium** = wrong figures or a blocked flow in a less common case; **low** = annoyance or edge case.

## Critical and high, in the order to fix them

1. **Sync push trusts a device token alone, plus a client-named staff member and approver.** `easalpha-api/app/controllers/api/v1/sync_controller.rb:62-66` lets `push` through with only a paired till token; `app/services/sync/push.rb:44-58` takes `record[:membership_id]` as the acting staff and `approved_by_membership_id` as a manager approval with no PIN; `/sync/changes` hands every membership id and permission list to any device-token holder. Copy `easalpha.device` from a till's localStorage (365-day token) and push refunds, voids, price overrides, shift closes and cash movements as the owner. CONFIRMED. Fix: require a cashier session for push, or sign each queued record with the session that made it; approvals offline need a PIN hash or an online approval before replay.

2. **Khata refund never reaches the journal.** `app/services/payments/refunder.rb:20-27` posts to `account&.post!` (khata has no payment account) and reduces the customer balance, but never calls `Finance::Journal`. After a full khata refund the journal still shows 1100 Dr / 4000 Cr; receivables and revenue stay overstated by every khata refund. CONFIRMED by probe.

3. **Expected cash ignores money that left the drawer without a cash movement.** `app/models/shift.rb:18-26` sums payments, refunds, customer payments and cash movements; expenses (`app/services/expenses/recorder.rb:22`), supplier payments (`purchasing/payments.rb:20`), purchase-return refunds (`purchasing/returns.rb:32`) and advances (`payroll/advances.rb:19`) post straight to `shift.drawer_account`. A Rs 300 petty expense makes the cashier Rs 300 short at blind close and the Z report disagrees with the drawer account. CONFIRMED by probe.

4. **Offline khata sale over the credit limit is lost at replay.** `app/services/payments/processor.rb:121-126` raises `khata.over_limit` regardless of `offline:`; `push.rb:76-88` has already created the order, so the record is rejected and the order sits unpaid with nothing receivable. The till does not block it: `easalpha-web/src/components/pos/payment-dialog.tsx:130` ignores `accountSummary(...).over` and only prints "The till will refuse it." CONFIRMED by probe.

5. **"Pay" on a staff meal online is refused.** `payment-dialog.tsx:125,131` lets Pay through for a staff meal with total zero and calls `onPay([], ...)`; `payOrder` posts `{ payments: [] }`; `processor.rb:23` raises `payment.required` (400). `/complete` works but the Pay button never calls it. Offline the local copy is marked paid while the server copy stays placed. CONFIRMED by probe. This is the flow the first client hit on 30 Oct.

6. **Receipt barcode does not fit 58 mm paper, and long branch codes overflow 80 mm on the browser and System paths.** `easalpha-desktop/src/escpos.ts:70-85` always sends `GS w 2` and code set B: "261030-MAIN-42" is 189 modules = 378 dots of 384, no quiet zone; a 3-digit serial or a longer branch code exceeds the print area and Epson-class firmware prints nothing. The browser SVG (`easalpha-web/src/lib/print.ts:302`) and the desktop System transport draw 0.33 mm a module: 61.7 mm inside a 48 mm body on 58 mm paper. No fallback for clone printers that do not accept the `{B` prefix. CONFIRMED by arithmetic. Fix: module width 1 on 58 mm or a digits-only code (yymmdd + serial in code set C), the SVG scaled to the body width, a per-printer "plain GS k 2" option.

7. **The cart, its customer and the staff-meal flag survive lock, sign-out and unpair.** `easalpha-web/src/store/cart.ts:111` persists the whole cart to localStorage; `src/store/device.ts:36,41` (`unpair`, `lock`) only null the session; `src/app/pos/pin/page.tsx:28` signs the next cashier in without `reset()`; `clearTillData` (`src/store/catalog.ts:89`) wipes IndexedDB but not `easalpha.cart` or the recent customers. Cashier B pays cashier A's order under B's name. CONFIRMED.

8. **Offline replay is not first-in first-out once a record is in backoff, and a rejected paid order looks paid and synced.** `src/lib/sync.ts:97-108`: a failed order gets `next_at` +4 s while its payment (a separate record, `pos-actions.ts:306-309`) pushes in 500 ms; the server answers `not_found` for the payment, the order then syncs unpaid while the till shows it paid. `sync.ts:142` `markRejected` keeps `status: "paid"` and clears `local_only`, so a sale rejected for a locked day or an archived product shows as paid and synced; Dismiss on the Attention page deletes the only trace. CONFIRMED.

9. **Voiding an in-transit transfer deletes the stock.** `app/services/stock/posting.rb:124-127` reverses ledger rows only `if doc.posted?`, but `dispatch!` already wrote `transfer_out`; `stock_documents_controller.rb:77-83` allows the cancel. Dispatch 10 of 100, cancel: level stays 90. CONFIRMED by test.

10. **The stock safety net loops forever on orders whose products have no recipe.** `app/services/stock/deduction.rb:53-60` sets `stock_deducted_at` only when entries exist; `safety_net.rb:10-12` keeps such orders as candidates for 14 days and writes a DeductionAudit each sweep (every 10 minutes). At 900 orders a day the audits table grows by roughly 100k rows a day. CONFIRMED by test.

11. **Payroll can recover an advance and the canteen bill twice; tips are shared business-wide.** `app/services/payroll/runner.rb:9` blocks a duplicate run only for the same `branch_id` (a branch run and an all-branches run both pass), `generate!` l.45-52 takes `outstanding_minor` without reserving it, `pay!` l.75-78 subtracts blindly; `runner.rb:21-22,43` `tips_total` has no branch filter. CONFIRMED by reading.

12. **A dispatched delivery order cannot be paid or edited from the till.** `app/models/order.rb:41` `editable?` omits `dispatched`; the till's `payOrder` (`src/lib/pos-actions.ts:257`) always saves before paying, so the cashier sees "Payment failed". Reopen also lands on `dispatched`. CONFIRMED by probe.

13. **A reopened order with a lower total gets stuck after the excess is refunded.** `refunder.rb:42` sets `payment_status = "partial"` for any non-full refund even when paid equals total; `/complete` then answers `order.unpaid` and `/payments` answers `payment.overpaid`. CONFIRMED by probe.

14. **An offline split payment with a tip doubles the tip on replay.** `pos-actions.ts:308` enqueues one record per split, each with the full `tip_minor`; `push.rb:127-131` applies it per record; `processor.rb:32` adds it each time. Two splits with tip 5,000: tip 10,000, order left partial on the server. CONFIRMED by probe.

15. **Pay can freeze for about 80 seconds when the Wi-Fi is up and the internet is down.** `src/lib/sync.ts:16-18` defines online as `navigator.onLine`; `pos-actions.ts:252` awaits a push with a 60 s timeout, then `saveOrder` waits another 20 s, before the offline path is used. CONFIRMED by reading.

## Medium

Ledger and finance: the day lock does not stop khata collections, expenses, manual journal vouchers, customer adjustments or payment-account deposits (`payments/khata.rb:10-21`, `expenses/recorder.rb:17-23`, `journal_entries_controller.rb:30`, `customer_accounts_controller.rb:22`, `payment_accounts_controller.rb:58-66`); any automatic voucher can be reversed from the API while the money stays (`journal_entries_controller.rb:35-40`); an expense void reverses into 5190 instead of its own category (`payment_account.rb:32-39`); drawer drops and payouts are booked to owner's equity 3010/3020 (`finance/journal.rb:44`, `shifts/manager.rb:62-70`); the sales journal posts tax, service charge, delivery, tips and rounding to 4000 Sales so Tax Payable never moves (`journal.rb:44,73-76`); refunds and late payments are dated to the order's business day, which changes an issued statement's closing balance (`refunder.rb:19`, `processor.rb:52`); re-paying a reopened order double-counts the customer's orders and spend (`processor.rb:137`); a code-vs-kind mismatch would split khata and cash figures if a second method of the same kind is added (`customers/ledger.rb:98`, `shift.rb:22`, `balances/rebuild.rb:40-45`).

Orders and payments: a part payment on an open order can be refunded, flipping it to `refunded` with no void and the ticket still in the kitchen (`refunder.rb:12`); the same coupon can be applied repeatedly on one order (`orders_controller.rb:264`); the total depends on the unordered iteration of `order.discounts` when fixed and percent discounts mix (`order.rb:18`, `pricing.rb`); `client_created_at` is unbounded, so orders land 90 days back or 30 days ahead with a fresh serial (`orders_controller.rb:163`, `push.rb`); one manager PIN unlocks every permission that manager holds for the whole request (`orders_controller.rb:347-351`); a payment id from another order makes the request a silent no-op (`processor.rb:19`); vouchers are not released on void.

Security: row-level security runs only when `EASALPHA_APP_DB_PASSWORD` is set and the compose file connects as `postgres` (`deploy/docker-compose.yml:25`, `easalpha-api/bin/start:14-17`); a storefront guest can claim any customer by phone and inherit their regular discount (`storefront/ordering.rb:27`); revoked devices and deactivated staff keep live ActionCable order streams and may pick any branch (`channels/application_cable/connection.rb:5-10`, `channel.rb:3`); no lockout on super admin or password login and `/api/v1/super/login` is outside the auth throttle (`super/sessions_controller.rb:5-9`, `rack_attack.rb:2`); QR and storefront ordering accept client-chosen order ids and sit outside the public throttle (`qr/ordering.rb:58`, `storefront/ordering.rb:33`, `rack_attack.rb:11-12`); a business owner can create or link a global user with a chosen password (`staff_controller.rb:28-29`, `me_controller.rb:26`); containers run as root, backups sit unencrypted on the same disk, no request-body limit (`Dockerfile`s, `deploy/backup.sh:25-26`, `deploy/Caddyfile.easalpha`); a locked till's device token opens the kitchen display with bump rights (`src/components/kitchen/auth.tsx:22`, `kitchen_controller.rb:75-76`).

Inventory, reports, canteen, payroll: refunds are counted differently by the sales report, the dashboard, COGS and commissions (`reports/sales.rb:10,17`, `dashboard.rb:47,55`, `stock.rb:63`, `commissions.rb:7`); a cancelled goods receipt leaves its price in the average cost and `value_minor` drifts from quantity times average (`stock/ledger.rb:20-22,31,46-48`); raising a quantity on a reopened order drops its packaging consumables and a failed re-deduct is never retried (`deduction.rb:11-23,60-63`, `transitions.rb:41`); the kiosk trusts any typed worker id and the sync ships every worker's fingerprint id, phone and CNIC (`canteen/claims.rb:10-11`, `canteen_controller.rb:61-69,183-189`, `canteen_serializers.rb:26-28`); offline claims trust the client time and session, and a rejected replay never reaches the kiosk (`claims.rb:9,14`, `push.rb:30-31`); a zero-net payslip skips the canteen settle and a cancelled claim is still deducted (`payroll/runner.rb:72-74`, `canteen/payroll.rb:17-24`).

Barcodes and scanner: price-embedded scale labels charge unit times a 3-decimal weight, not the label price (Rs 50 label becomes Rs 50.40; `src/lib/barcode.ts:424`); the receipt-number pattern fails for a branch code with a space and `_`/`%` in a code are ILIKE wildcards (`branch.rb:5-6`, `receipt-format.ts:46`, `orders_controller.rb:28`); a product Code 128 shaped like `\d{6}-\S+-\d+` can never be sold by scanning because the receipt check runs first (`scanner.tsx:210`); `?receipt=<text>` with a non-receipt value opens whichever order the ILIKE search returns first (`src/app/pos/orders/page.tsx:70`).

Till: non-network push failures (revoked device, 403) back off forever with the badge still green (`sync.ts:106-108`); the kitchen display shows stale orders silently when the API is down (`src/app/kitchen/page.tsx:151`); auto-lock is hard-coded to 5 minutes (`device.ts:33`); F4 and F5 bypass `busy` and open dialogs, so a double F5 sends twice and shows a false error (`src/app/pos/page.tsx:371-373`).

## Low

A scan into a focused Amount field becomes a tendered amount of billions (`payment-dialog.tsx:163`); the Orders page scanner is off during the initial refresh and while an order is open; UPC-E is never expanded and EAN-8 zero-padded to 13 is not reduced; label modules can go below one dot on the 38 mm roll and Helvetica cannot draw Urdu names; `loadSymbol`'s cache is unbounded. Two tabs of the till break each other's session (one-use refresh tokens); `nextTillSerial` is read-then-write; `openByReceipt` stores a summary row with empty lines; the service worker is cache-first on `/_next/static/` with a hand-bumped version; private-mode browsers leave the Refresh button spinning. Ledger: a reopened order whose total drops below `paid_minor` can be completed with the excess unrecorded; staff meals count their subtotal in `Finance::ProfitLoss`; `customer_id` can change on a partly khata-paid order; customer-payment numbers can collide across customers. Reports and kitchen: the KDS loads every open order across all days with an N+1 and polls every 10 s per screen, and shows "Bump order" on ready cards that the server refuses; stock counts read `expected` unlocked and against today's level even when backdated; `MealSession.max_claims` is never enforced and `Plans.copy!` creates drafts nothing publishes; the receiving branch cannot see an incoming transfer. Security: any super admin can mint tokens for any approved support session; cable and print-server tokens are accepted in query strings; the print server allows any origin and compares the token with `===`; CSV exports are unescaped; `otp_request` reveals whether an account exists; `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN` defaults to "easalpha"; the till lock is client-only; PIN throttle counters live in the file cache and reset with the container.

## Found sound

Tenancy (default scope, `NoTenantError`, row-level policies, scoped finds in every sampled controller); JWTs with expiry and per-request checks of membership status, business deletion and device revocation; rotated, hashed refresh tokens; OTP and pairing limits; webhooks fail closed with constant-time compares; prices and discounts computed server-side; refunds capped at what was captured; day lock on pay, refund, void and order create/edit; serials by atomic upsert; payment ids fixed before sending and deduplicated on replay; Pay disabled while loading and the server refusing overpay; the "server took the payment but answered late" path; `Finance::Journal.post!` enforcing balanced entries and immutable rows; customer balances written under a row lock by all four writers and matching the SQL sum; statements with inclusive business-day ranges; till and server pricing agreeing on tax per line, inclusive tax, discount allocation, service charge base, whole-rupee and cash rounding; stock sale rows unique per line so a retry never deducts twice; deals and modifiers deducting their recipes; meal claims one per session by partial unique index; the rider 15-minute sign-out fix; keyboard-wedge prefix, suffix and terminator handling; label quiet zones and EAN check digits; the orders search parameterised.

## Debit side: students who buy on account and pay at month or semester end

Reviewed on request the same day. The customer-account module was designed for this (12 Oct 2026 decisions: opening balances for students who already owe, guardian phones for hostel students, member number and room, customer groups for hostel blocks, tuck-shop barcodes).

**What works for it.** Buying on account at the till with a per-student credit limit (no limit means unlimited); opening balances and corrections that are insert-only, need a reason and a permission or PIN, and post a voucher; a statement for any period with the balance brought forward and a running balance (`app/services/customers/ledger.rb`), so a semester statement is one request with from and to; bills with the items, as PDF, on WhatsApp to the student and the guardian, and as a public link; automatic monthly bills from a chosen day with a due date and a minimum balance; a manual bill run for any period; payments of any amount at any time by cash, card, bank or wallet, an overpayment becoming a credit the till shows as "in credit"; a WhatsApp "payment received" message; aging in current, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and over 90 days with payments allocated oldest first; customers listed by balance.

**Gaps and defects for this use, in order.**

1. **Nobody can take a student's payment at the till.** Collection exists only in Admin, Customers, Payments (`easalpha-web/src/app/(admin)/admin/customers/payments/page.tsx`); the till has no screen for it (`src/app/pos/` holds orders, shift, delivery, campaign, qr, attention) and the default cashier role lacks `khata.collect` (`app/models/permission.rb:93`, CUSTOMER_ACCOUNT off by default). At a canteen counter the student who comes to pay must wait for someone with the admin panel. High for this business.
2. **No collection while offline.** The sync push accepts orders, payments, refunds, voids, shifts, cash movements and meal claims only (`app/services/sync/push.rb:5`); `Payments::Khata.receive!` has no queued form. With the internet down nothing can be recorded against an account, and the admin panel is down too. High.
3. **No printed receipt for an account payment.** `lib/print.ts` prints order receipts and Z reports only; the admin Payments page has no print. A student paying Rs 5,000 leaves with nothing in hand unless a phone is linked. High.
4. **Offline sales over the limit are lost at sync, and the till's cached balance is stale** (item 4 in the main list; `offline-customers.ts` adds the till's own sales but never other tills'). The limit is the only control, and it does not hold offline. High.
5. **A refund on an account sale never reaches the journal, and it is dated to the sale's day** (ledger items 2 and 8 above). A returned item in November lands on the September statement that was already sent, so the semester statement and the issued bills disagree. High.
6. **A payment cannot be dated.** `Payments::Khata.receive!` stamps `business_day_for(Time.current)` (`app/services/payments/khata.rb:11`); a bank transfer received yesterday, or entered after midnight for today, lands on the wrong day of the statement. Medium.
7. **Nothing happens after the due date.** The bill carries a due date (`khata.monthly_bills.due_days`), but there is no reminder after it, no aging-based message, and no rule such as "no more credit when a bill is 30 days overdue"; `Customers::Notify` has account_receipt, account_bill, adjustment, opening and payment-received only. Medium.
8. **The till identifies a student by typing.** Search is by name, phone, khata number or member number (`src/components/pos/customer-search.tsx`); the scanner knows products and receipt numbers only, so a student ID card with a barcode cannot be scanned to pick the account. Medium for a busy counter.
9. **The public bill link dies after 120 days** (`app/services/customers/bill_token.rb:7`); a semester bill sent in February cannot be opened in July. The PDF attached to WhatsApp still works. Low.
10. **Re-paying a reopened order double-counts the student's orders and spend** (money item 6 above), which feeds the regular-customer tiers. Low.

**Suggested shape of the fix.** A "Receive payment" button on the till's customer dialog that prints a receipt and queues a `customer_payment` record for sync (with the same idempotent id pattern as payments); a scanned member barcode on the customer field; an overdue reminder job reusing the bill templates, plus an optional "hold credit when overdue" setting; a `received_on` date on collections for managers; the journal and dating fixes from the ledger list.
